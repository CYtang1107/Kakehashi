# KAKEHASHI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Older adults are the primary audience. Family, visiting students, helpers and public story readers use existing role screens. The prototype uses an illustrative household, not authenticated accounts.

## Product Purpose

Regular companionship, memories and a safer home while the older adult controls decisions. Visits, stories and safer rooms matter more than removal of belongings.

## Operating Context

React Native, Expo Router, TypeScript, Expo Web and Express in one workspace. English and Japanese. Preserve routes, APIs, consent, reports, media, house views, helpers and demo payments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Stage 1 changes frontend foundations only. No AI, matching, device integration or new backend. Reading preferences apply across roles, as confirmed by the user. Shared controls adopt them now; inline screen typography, native preference persistence and dark appearance are future work.

## Brand Commitments

Keep KAKEHASHI, senior autonomy, Not now, family wishes rather than votes, recording/sharing consent and human report review. The user requests ivory, sage and deep green, warm welcoming design, readable text and spacious labelled controls.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing code, Stage 0 audits and illustrative data. No real-account, AI-matching or device-monitoring claims. Web evidence cannot certify native usability.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Keep decisions and sharing under the older adult's control.
- Preserve meaning and workflows while changing presentation.
- Identify demonstrations and provide recoverable feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large English/Japanese typography, native font scaling, generous targets, contrast, focus and screen-reader names. Normal/Large/Extra Large, high contrast and reduced motion never disable OS accessibility settings.
